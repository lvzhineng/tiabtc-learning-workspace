# TiaBTC Learning and Review

This context defines the language shared by video learning, candlestick review, free replay, chart drawings, and simulated trading in the local TiaBTC workspace.

## Learning

**Learning Video**:
A published TiaBTC video that can be tracked by learning status and opened in a review workspace at its publication time.
_Avoid_: Trade, Replay, Chart Record

**Learning State**:
The current progress of a Learning Video: unlearned, learning, or learned, together with the time it was last changed.
_Avoid_: Review Progress, Playback Progress

## Review

**Review Workspace**:
The shared candlestick workspace used by every review mode, including timeframe selection, navigation, drawings, and simulated trading.
_Avoid_: Video Dialog, Trade Chart, Replay Page

**Review Mode**:
The reason and disclosure rules under which a Review Workspace is opened. The supported modes are Video Review, Free Replay, and optionally Trade Review.
_Avoid_: Page Type, Chart Type

**Video Review**:
A Review Mode anchored to a Learning Video's publication time, with later candlesticks hidden until replay reveals them.
_Avoid_: Video Chart, Historical Chart

**Free Replay**:
A Review Mode anchored to a user-selected symbol and start time, with future candlesticks hidden until explicitly revealed.
_Avoid_: Backtest, Video Replay

**Trade Review**:
An optional Review Mode anchored to a completed imported trade, showing that trade's entry and exit markers.
_Avoid_: Free Replay, Paper Trade

**Review Anchor**:
The market time and source identity that establish the initial position of a Review Workspace.
_Avoid_: Cursor, Current Candle, Start Date

**Replay Cursor**:
The latest candlestick currently revealed by a replay-capable Review Mode.
_Avoid_: Review Anchor, Mouse Cursor, Visible Range

**Replay Progress**:
The revealed market-time boundary of a replay session. It survives timeframe changes within the active page but is not learning progress.
_Avoid_: Learning State, Review Progress

## Market Data

**Market Data Source**:
The CCXT-backed exchange provider that supplies candlesticks and symbol validation. The current provider is Bybit USDT perpetual.
_Avoid_: OKX, Price Provider

**Symbol**:
A Bybit USDT perpetual contract identifier such as `BTCUSDT`.
_Avoid_: Instrument, Pair, `BTC-USDT-SWAP`

**Review Timeframe**:
A supported candlestick interval used by the Review Workspace: `1`, `5`, `15`, `60`, `240`, `D`, or `W`.
_Avoid_: OKX Bar, Display Label

**Candlestick Cache**:
The local SQLite copy of Bybit candlesticks and known covered ranges. It is reusable derived data, not a second market data source.
_Avoid_: Market Archive, Source of Truth

**Offline Mode**:
A user setting that restricts market-data reads to the Candlestick Cache and prevents Bybit requests.
_Avoid_: Replay Mode, Disconnected Session

## Review Content

**Chart Drawing**:
A persisted price/time annotation belonging to a Symbol and visible across review modes and timeframes according to its tool geometry.
_Avoid_: Video Drawing, Temporary Overlay

**System Marker**:
A non-user annotation generated by a Review Mode, such as a video publication marker or imported trade entry and exit markers.
_Avoid_: Chart Drawing

**Paper Trade**:
A simulated position created inside a Review Workspace and persisted independently from imported historical trades.
_Avoid_: Trade Review, Imported Trade, Order

**Imported Trade**:
A completed historical trade read from an external workbook for Trade Review. The source workbook remains read-only.
_Avoid_: Paper Trade, Excel Row
